2 How and When to Run the 6 Core Monthly GSC Reports

When to Run
  • Timing: First business week of each month, specifically on or after the 3rd or 4th of the month.
  • Why the 3-day buffer is required: Google Search Console takes 48 to 72 hours to finalize and aggregate search queries, clicks, and impressions. Running on the 1st of the month will miss the final 2-3 days of data. Running on the 3rd or 4th guarantees 100% complete calendar month metrics.
  • Frequency: Once monthly as part of the digital monthly close.
How to Run
  • Recommended Batch Execution: Use run-monthly-reports.py with --reports-file report-lists/core-reports.txt to execute the 6 core reports across all 15 sites in one automated run.
  • Execution Volume: 15 sites × 6 core reports = 90 executions (instead of 420 for all reports).
  • Dry-Run Verification: Always use --dry-run first if you wish to verify the command queue without making API requests.
Command: Run Core Monthly Suite Across All Sites

Campaign & UTM Tracking Reports in GA4

GSC vs GA4 Architecture Note: Google Search Console does not track UTM parameters. GSC canonicalises all search URLs to their clean root, stripping out all query strings, UTM tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content), and paid click tokens (gclid, msclkid). GA4 is the single source of truth for UTM and marketing campaign performance.

Within ga4-api-explorer, two purpose-built scripts isolate and evaluate UTM parameters:

1. utm_campaign_report.py
Macro Campaign Attribution
  • Dimensions: sessionCampaignName, sessionSourceMedium
  • Metrics: Sessions, Engaged Sessions, Engagement Rate, Conversions
  • Best For: High-level commercial campaign pacing, evaluating which marketing initiatives drive the most qualified sessions and conversions.
2. utm_full_content_report.py
Granular Ad-Creative & Landing Page
  • Dimensions: sessionCampaignName, sessionManualAdContent (utm_content), landingPagePlusQueryString
  • Metrics: Sessions, Engaged Sessions, Engagement Rate, Bounce Rate, Conversions
  • Noise Stripping: Automatically scrubs ad click IDs (gclid, msclkid, fbclid), Marketo tokens (mkt_tok), HubSpot tags (_hsenc), and strips utm_* parameters from the landing URL to prevent row fragmentation while preserving dimension integrity.
