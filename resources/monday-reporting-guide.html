Overview & Windows Environment Setup

Every Monday morning, two vital reporting operations are run to monitor organic search health, content distribution, and overall portfolio stability across our digital properties.

Report 1: Accountancy Daily Editorial

Focus: Content Performance & Editorial Strategy

Analyses story reach, click volume, and viral distribution across Google Web Search, Google Discover, and Google News over the past 7 days. Helps editors know exactly which articles won the week.

Report 2: Weekly Canary Monitoring Suite

Focus: Triage & Anomaly Detection (11 Properties)

A triple-report early warning system across both Google Search Console (GSC) and Google Analytics 4 (GA4). Detects algorithmic drops, tracking drop-offs, and shifts across WoW (Week-on-Week), MoM (Month-on-Month), and YoY (Year-on-Year).

2 Portfolio Canary Monitoring Suite (GA4 & GSC)

What We Learn From The Canary Suite

A "canary" report acts as our early warning radar across 11 key group properties (Croner, Croner-i, Accountancy Daily, HR-inform, Employer Advice, My VIP Tax Team, Croner Taxwise). It alerts us to:

  • Technical & Tagging Outages: Sudden portfolio-wide drops in GA4 sessions or GSC impressions signal broken tracking containers or deployment regressions.
  • Algorithmic & Crawling Fluctuations: Significant WoW shifts in GSC clicks and average positions highlight Google search algorithm volatility or indexing issues.
  • Channel & Campaign Shifts: By comparing medium-level and source-level traffic, we isolate whether traffic shifts stem from paid campaigns, marketing emails, referral changes, or organic search.
  • Tri-Horizon Health: Shows performance across Week-on-Week (WoW), Month-on-Month (MoM), and Year-on-Year (YoY) simultaneously to contextualise seasonal fluctuations.

The 3 Required Reports Explained

1. GSC Canary

Script: canary_report.py

Repo: gsc-page-exporter

Organic search health across all 11 properties. Reports clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position vs previous week & month.

2. GA4 Canary (Medium)

Script: weekly_canary_html.py

Repo: ga4-api-explorer

Total active users and traffic split by channel medium (organic, direct, referral, email) vs last week, last month, and YoY.

3. GA4 Canary (Source / Medium)

Script: weekly_canary_html.py --source

Repo: ga4-api-explorer

Granular view breaking down specific traffic sources (Google organic, Bing, newsletter tags, partner referral domains).

How to Read the Canary Reports & Draft Commentary

The email commentary is your opportunity to synthesise the data so directors and stakeholders can digest the state of our digital estate in 30 seconds.

Understanding the Colour Thresholds
Critical / Red Drop (≤ -25%): Immediate investigation required. Could indicate broken tracking, 404 errors, indexing de-listings, or significant search algorithm drops.
Warning / Amber (-10% to -25%): Moderate dip. Often seasonal (e.g. half-terms, bank holidays) or routine ranking fluctuations.
Neutral / Grey (-10% to +10%): Stable expected operational baseline.
Positive / Green (≥ +10%): Notable uptick in users or organic visibility (campaign launch, seasonal surge, or viral news story).
Triage Flowchart for Writing Commentary
  1. Open the GSC Canary: Check the Portfolio Health summary box at the top. Note how many properties are Critical, Warning, or Stable.
  2. Correlate with GA4 Medium: If a property is red in GSC, is it also red in GA4 Organic Medium? If yes, it is an actual organic visibility decline. If GA4 is green/stable, other channels may be compensating.
  3. Drill into GA4 Source/Medium: If a property has a severe drop, inspect the source table to see if it is isolated to Google Organic, Direct (possible tracking drop), or Email (campaign hiatus).
  4. Synthesise: Write 2 to 4 actionable bullets highlighting the most impactful trends.

Troubleshooting & FAQs

If a script returns an HttpError 401: Invalid Credentials, your local OAuth refresh token may have expired. Delete the local cache file (e.g. .cache/token.json or token.pickle) and re-run the script. A Google OAuth browser prompt will appear asking you to authorise access.

Google Search Console takes 48 to 72 hours to finalize and aggregate search queries, clicks, and impressions. The scripts use smart date detection to automatically anchor to the latest complete available date, ensuring we never compare incomplete partial days.

Ensure your virtual environment is active (source .venv/Scripts/activate). If a package like jinja2 or google-analytics-data is missing, install the requirements file using: pip install -r requirements.txt.
