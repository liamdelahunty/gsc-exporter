Methodology and Scoring Definitions
Comp. Score (Composite Score)
Method A

Formula: Clicks + (Impressions / 50.0)

Balances actual organic traffic (1 point per click) with latent audience reach (1 point per 50 search impressions). Candidates in this report are sorted primarily by this score so that content teams prioritise high-traffic drivers alongside high-visibility pages that offer immediate SEO growth potential.

Rank Sum (Combined Rank Sum)
Method B

Formula: Rank(Clicks) + Rank(Impressions)

An unweighted relative benchmark where each URL is independently ranked across all legacy candidates by clicks and impressions (using minimum ranking). Lower numerical values denote superior overall standing across both metrics (for example, rank #1 in clicks and rank #1 in impressions yields a Rank Sum of 2).

Tier (Tiered Allocation)
Method C

Formula: Tier 1 (Top Clicks) • Tier 2 (Top Impr) • Tier 3 (Long Tail)

A segmented quota model guaranteeing the migration captures proven traffic drivers (Tier 1: top 700 by clicks) alongside high latent demand opportunities (Tier 2: next top 300 by impressions from remaining candidates). Any remaining inventory is designated as Tier 3 long tail for phased or conditional review.
